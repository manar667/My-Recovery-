    General nutrition tips

      BMI Calculator

      Important: weight vs. swelling

      Lymphedema can add weight that is not fat. If your weight changes quickly (within days) or one arm feels heavier, this may be swelling and not real weight gain. Contact your doctor before changing your diet.

      More notes

        About

        App information and disclaimer

        What this app does

        My Recovery helps you follow your home exercise programme, understand warning signs, and record your daily symptoms after breast surgery. All data is stored only on your own device.

        Medical disclaimer

        This application is an educational tool created for an academic project. It does not provide a diagnosis, does not prescribe medication, and is not a substitute for your surgeon, oncologist, or physiotherapist. Always follow your medical team's instructions.
